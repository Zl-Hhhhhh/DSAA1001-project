# Assessment 1: modelling plan

**Target and baseline.** Predict Fail = 1 versus Pass or Pass w/ Conditions = 0. Retain unjudged visits as earlier history. Compare an always-non-Fail rule with regularized Logistic Regression. The pipeline uses training medians for missing numbers, adds missing-value indicators and encodes categories as binary columns, including handling for unseen categories. Inputs are facility type, risk, inspection purpose, ZIP, month/weekday and coordinates. Raw identifiers and names serve record matching; current Results, Violations and its missingness flag are excluded from predictors.

**Improvements to test.** Treat risk as separate categories and group categories appearing fewer than 20 times in training. Add earlier visit, judgement and failure counts, failure proportions and last-result status. Represent absent and mixed history explicitly. Test whether history has different effects for different inspection purposes. Visit counts use strictly earlier records. Interpret ZIP differences as associations.

**Time split and selection.** Train on 2016-2024 (140,762 labelled rows), validate on 2025 (16,060), and test once on January-September 2026 (10,515). Reserve partial October data for the final refit. Within training, use data through 2021 to predict 2022, through 2022 to predict 2023, and through 2023 to predict 2024. Compare C in {0.1, 1, 10} and class_weight in {None, balanced} using mean Macro-F1 at threshold 0.5. Use 2025 to choose a threshold from 0.10 to 0.80 in steps of 0.05. Fix features, settings and threshold, then retrain through 2025 for the local test. Use random seed 1001.

**History and repeated businesses.** Build training history from earlier dates, grouping same-day records first. Each validation/test batch uses visits and outcomes from before its start date. This matches the hidden-test setup; annual evaluation batches use older history than its 28-day period. Unjudged visits add to visit counts; failure rates and last-result status use judged visits. Compare licence grouping with licence-plus-address grouping. Define known businesses using any visit before the batch starts and report known/unseen scores separately; about 85% of evaluation rows concern known groups. A business can appear on both sides of a TimeSeriesSplit.

**Evaluation and limits.** Report Macro-F1 with labels [0, 1] and zero_division=0, precision/recall/F1 for each class, and a confusion matrix. False negatives miss failures; false positives flag non-failing inspections. Examine errors by year, purpose and known/unseen status with the model fixed. The local test was selected from existing data whose labels had already been checked during preparation; outcome analysis in this EDA covers 2016-2025. Inspection selection, imperfect business matching, missing pre-2016 history and the 2018 procedure change limit interpretation.

**Final prediction.** After local evaluation, retrain the fixed model on the frozen snapshot through 2026-10-09. Record the threshold and data version. Use only the teaching team's pre-visit inputs for hidden-test prediction and export integer predictions by Inspection ID. Keep inputs and code available offline.
