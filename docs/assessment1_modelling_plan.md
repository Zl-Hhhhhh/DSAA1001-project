# Assessment 1: modelling plan

**Target and baseline.** Predict Fail = 1 versus Pass or Pass w/ Conditions = 0. Use visits without a judgement only as earlier history. Compare an always-non-Fail rule with regularized Logistic Regression. Use a scikit-learn pipeline to fill missing numbers with training medians, add missing-value indicators and encode categories as binary columns, with handling for unseen categories. Inputs are facility type, risk, inspection purpose, ZIP, month/weekday and coordinates. Exclude raw identifiers, names, current Results, Violations and its missingness flag.

**Improvements to test.** Treat risk as separate categories. Group categories appearing fewer than 20 times in training. Add earlier visit/judgement counts, failure proportions and last-result status, keeping missing or mixed history explicit. Test whether history has different effects for different inspection purposes. Exclude full-period visit totals. Check errors by location without treating ZIP differences as causal.

**Time split and selection.** Train on 2016-2024 (140,762 labelled rows), validate on 2025 (16,060), and test once on January-September 2026 (10,515). Exclude incomplete October. Within training, use data through 2021 to predict 2022, through 2022 to predict 2023, and through 2023 to predict 2024. Compare C in {0.1, 1, 10} and class_weight in {None, balanced} using mean Macro-F1 at threshold 0.5. Use 2025 to choose a probability threshold from 0.10 to 0.80 in steps of 0.05. Fix features, settings and threshold before testing. Retrain through 2025 for the local test; use random seed 1001.

**History and repeated businesses.** Build training history from earlier dates, grouping same-day records first. For each validation/test batch, use only outcomes from before the batch starts. One prediction must not use another outcome from that batch. This matches the hidden test, though annual evaluation batches leave history older than in its 28-day period. Count earlier unjudged visits. Compare licence grouping with licence-plus-address grouping. Report known/unseen scores based on records before each period starts; about 85% of evaluation rows concern known groups. TimeSeriesSplit does not separate business groups.

**Evaluation and limits.** Report Macro-F1 with labels [0, 1] and zero_division=0, precision/recall/F1 for each class, and a confusion matrix. False negatives miss failures; false positives direct attention to non-failing inspections. Examine errors by year, purpose and known/unseen status without adjusting the model to test outcomes. The local test was selected from existing data whose labels were checked during preparation. This EDA excludes its outcome patterns. Non-random inspections, imperfect business matching, missing pre-2016 history and the 2018 procedure change limit interpretation.

**Final prediction.** After the local evaluation and final lock, retrain on saved history available by 2026-10-30. Record the threshold and data version. Generate integer predictions by Inspection ID using the teaching team's inputs. Never query hidden outcomes. Keep inputs and code available offline.
